Delete Your Account

What gets deleted

Deleting your Heaps Arboriculture Surveyors account:

  • Permanently removes your name, email, phone number, home postcode, insurance (PI/PL) and DBS details, and uploaded certificates
  • Signs you out and stops you being able to log in again
  • Keeps job and payment history on file, as we're required to for our business records, but with no name or contact details attached to it

This can't be undone.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

1Open the Heaps Arboriculture Surveyors app and go to Profile
2Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
3Confirm — your account and personal data are deleted straight away

Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address registered on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days.
