The job journey, status by status

Every job carries one dispatch status, shown as a coloured dot on the jobs list, the dashboard and the live map. This page walks through each status: what it means, what moves the job on, who gets an email, and what the surveyors can see.

How a job starts

There are two ways in. Either way the job appears straight away in the CRM as Pending approval.

A customer uses the website form

  1. The customer fills in the enquiry form. The enquiry and its job are created together.
  2. You send the quote from the CRM. The customer gets the price by email.
  3. The customer accepts with one click, then fills in their full details. The job shows Details Received.
  4. If they decline instead, the job is archived and you get an email with their reason.
to you on a new enquiry, on acceptance and on decline; to the customer with the quote and a "confirm your details" link.

You add it yourself

  1. Use New job on the Jobs or Clients page, or Add job on a customer's page.
  2. Pick an existing customer or add a new one in the same form. Customers are matched by email, so nobody is added twice.
  3. Choose the survey type, site postcode and deadline. The urgency and SLA date are worked out for you.
  4. Start it as Pending approval, or as Waiting for plans if you're still waiting on drawings.
to you: "Job created, ready for preparation".

Each status in turn

Who does what at every stage, and what happens on its own.

Waiting for plans Parked until the architect's revised plans arrive. Not on any map, and the SLA clock isn't running.

Typically

A Stage 2 BS5837 job (AIA, AMS, TPP), made from the Stage 1 job with Create Stage 2 Job.

Moves on when

Plans arrive and you press Plans Received, Activate Job.

What happens

  • The SLA clock starts from today.
  • The job moves to Pending approval.
Pending approval The job exists, but surveyors can't see it yet.

Office does

  • Prepare the Axiscape survey database.
  • Draft the initial report template.
  • Check the site address, trees and fee.

Moves on when

You press Approve to Map on the job page.

What happens

  • The surveyor's pay is worked out (hours × their rate).
  • A red dot appears on the surveyor map.
to you, confirming the job is live.
Unallocated Live on the map. Any active surveyor whose coverage area includes the site can see and claim it.

The job on the app's map and its Available list, if the site is within their travel radius.

Moves on when

What happens

The job turns orange and leaves everyone else's map.

to you when it's claimed.

Claimed One surveyor owns the job and is arranging the visit.

  • Visits the site.

Works down the job page's Report checklist:

  • Axiscape DB prepared

Report to generate and Report generated tick-boxes. They feed the dashboard's Reports box and decide when Invoice 2 is due on a 50/50 job.

to you, confirming, with a reminder to invoice.
Report sent The report is out. The job leaves the surveyor's app. It's waiting on invoicing.

Office does

Raise the invoice in QuickBooks, then record it on the job (see Invoicing below).

Moves on when

The job is fully invoiced: one invoice, or both on a 50/50 job.

What happens

It archives itself. This works in either order, so if you invoiced before the report went out, ticking "report sent" archives it.

Archived Finished: report sent and fully invoiced, or the quote was declined.

Where it lives

The Archived filter on the Jobs page. Hidden from the default list and every map.

Payment

Marking an invoice paid doesn't change the status. Archived jobs still show paid and unpaid invoices.

Overdue

An invoice unpaid after 30 days is flagged overdue on the job.

Urgency and the SLA deadline

Urgency comes from the deadline chosen on the form (or on New job). The SLA date is that many working days ahead, Monday to Friday.

UrgencyDeadlineSurcharge on the quote
Urgent3 working days£200
Elevated5 working days£150
Standard10 working days£75
Low15 working days or moreAs quoted

Changing urgency on the job page restarts the SLA from today. That's also how an imported job gets an SLA for the first time.

The dashboard's Overdue box counts jobs past their SLA date whose report hasn't been sent.

Invoicing

Invoices are raised in QuickBooks. The CRM records them so the dashboard totals, overdue flags and archiving all work.

Single invoice (the default)

  • Mark Invoiced records one invoice for the agreed fee, dated today.
  • Custom Invoice lets you type a different amount or date.
  • Mark Invoice Paid records the payment date.

50/50 split

  • Invoice 1: surveyor cost, half the survey fee and any urgency fee.
  • Invoice 2: the other half of the survey fee, once the report is generated. The CRM asks you to confirm if you raise it before then.
  • Each invoice is marked paid separately.

Switch between Single and 50/50 in the Financial section of the job page. The dashboard's This month and This year figures add up invoices raised (excluding VAT), not payments received.
