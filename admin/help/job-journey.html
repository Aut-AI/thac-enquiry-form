The job journey, stage by stage

Every job is at one stage, shown on the jobs list, the dashboard and the live map. This page walks through each stage: what it means, what moves the job on, who gets an email, and what the surveyors can see. On the map a red pin is a job nobody has yet, and a green pin is a job a surveyor has taken.

How a job starts

There are two ways in. Either way, once it's a real job it goes straight onto the map as Unallocated. There's no approval step.

A customer accepts a quote on the website

  1. The customer fills in the enquiry form. The enquiry and its job are created together. The job waits off the map as Awaiting go-ahead.
  2. You send the quote from the CRM. The customer gets the price by email.
  3. The customer accepts with one click. The job goes straight onto the map as Unallocated. They then fill in their full details, and the job shows Details Received.
  4. If they decline instead, the job is archived and you get an email with their reason.
to you on a new enquiry, on acceptance ("create the database") and on decline; to the customer with the quote and a "confirm your details" link.

You add it yourself

  1. Use New job on the Jobs or Clients page, or Add job on a customer's page.
  2. Pick an existing customer or add a new one in the same form. Customers are matched by email, so nobody is added twice.
  3. Choose the survey type, site postcode and deadline. The urgency and SLA date are worked out for you.
  4. It starts on the map as Unallocated, or as Waiting for plans if you're still waiting on drawings.
to you: "New job on map, database to create".

Your two to-dos on every job

From the moment a job is on the map, the job page, the jobs list and the dashboard's To Do box show two tick-boxes.

Database: to create → digital plan, or JPEG

The most important one. Nothing can happen until the database goes to the surveyor. Urgent jobs first. When you create it, say what it was made from: Digital plan, or JPEG, awaiting digital plan. JPEG jobs stay on the dashboard's To Do box under Awaiting digital plan until you switch them to Digital plan. When a surveyor takes a job whose database isn't ready, you're emailed to create it now, and the dashboard shows it under Database needed, surveyor waiting.

Report to draft → Report drafted

Tick it whenever you draft the report. You can do it early, but it matters less than the database.

Each stage in turn

Who does what at every stage, and what happens on its own.

Awaiting go-ahead Off the map. A website quote not yet accepted, or a job you took off the map.

Typically

The customer hasn't accepted the quote yet. Amendment requests also wait here, since they need no site visit.

Moves on when

  • The customer accepts the quote, or
  • you press Put on Map on the job page.

What happens

The job goes on the map as Unallocated.

Waiting for plans Off the map until the architect's revised plans arrive. The SLA clock isn't running.

Typically

A Stage 2 BS5837 job (AIA, AMS, TPP), made from the Stage 1 job with Create Stage 2 Job, or a job you added as Waiting for plans.

Moves on when

Plans arrive and you press Plans Received, Activate Job.

What happens

  • The SLA clock starts from today.
  • The job goes straight onto the map as Unallocated.
Unallocated A red pin on the map. Any active surveyor whose area includes the site can see and claim it.

Office does

  • Create the database (urgent jobs first).
  • Draft the report if there's time.

Moves on when

  • A surveyor taps Claim in the app and accepts the NDA. If they click it, they've got it. There's no approval, or
  • you Allocate to Surveyor and they accept.

What happens

The pin turns green and leaves everyone else's map.

to you: "Job allocated, create the database now" (or "send the database" if it's ready).

Going back: Remove from Map takes the job off the map as Awaiting go-ahead.

Allocated A green pin. One surveyor has the job and is arranging the visit.

Office does

Get the database to the surveyor.

Surveyor does

  • Agrees the survey date and visits the site.
  • Back at the office, taps Survey Done in the app.

What happens

The pin leaves the map. The job shows Survey done, awaiting data from surveyor.

Going back: the surveyor can hand the job back, or you can press Unassign Surveyor. Either way it's back on the map as Unallocated, and you're emailed.

Survey done The site visit is done. Waiting for the surveyor's data.

Surveyor does

  • Tidies the database.
  • Puts it, with photos and site notes, in SharePoint.
  • Taps Data Sent in the app.

Moves on when

The surveyor taps Data Sent.

What happens

The job shows Survey data received, report to complete.

to you: "Survey data received".
Data received The data is in SharePoint. The job is ready for you to finalise.

Office does

Picks up the data and finishes the job one of three ways, from Finish the job on the job page.

Finish the job

  • Report & Invoice Sent: records the invoice. Job complete.
  • Tree Data Sent, Part Invoiced: records the part invoice. Awaiting client.
  • Tree Data Sent, Not Invoiced: Awaiting client.

Note

Raise the invoice in QuickBooks first. The CRM records it for the dashboard totals and overdue flags. Sending the report and invoicing are one step.

Awaiting client Tree data sent. Waiting on more information from the client, for example their plans. Shown as part invoiced or not invoiced.

Where it lives

Off the map, but still in the jobs list (the Awaiting Client tab) and on the dashboard. Its SLA is paused.

Moves on when

The client comes back and you press Report & Invoice Sent.

What happens

If it was part invoiced, the final invoice is the balance (it's filled in for you). The job is complete.

Job complete Report and invoice sent. (A declined quote shows as Archived instead.)

Where it lives

The Complete tab on the Jobs page. Hidden from the default list and every map.

Payment

Mark each invoice paid on the job page when the money arrives. That doesn't change the stage.

Overdue

An invoice unpaid after 30 days is flagged overdue on the job.

Urgency and the SLA deadline

Urgency comes from the deadline chosen on the form (or on New job). The SLA date is that many working days ahead, Monday to Friday.

UrgencyDeadlineSurcharge on the quote
Urgent3 working days£200
Elevated5 working days£150
Standard10 working days£75
Low15 working days or moreAs quoted

Changing urgency on the job page restarts the SLA from today. That's also how an imported job gets an SLA for the first time.

The dashboard's Overdue box counts jobs past their SLA date that aren't finished, leaving out jobs awaiting the client.

Invoicing

Invoices are raised in QuickBooks. The CRM records them, through the finish buttons, so the dashboard totals and overdue flags work.

Invoiced in one go

  • Report & Invoice Sent records one invoice (the agreed fee is filled in; change it if needed) and completes the job.
  • Mark Invoice Paid records the payment date.

Part invoiced

  • Tree Data Sent, Part Invoiced records invoice 1 (half the fee is filled in; change it if needed).
  • Report & Invoice Sent later records invoice 2 for the balance and completes the job.
  • Each invoice is marked paid separately.

The dashboard's This month and This year figures add up invoices raised (excluding VAT), not payments received.
