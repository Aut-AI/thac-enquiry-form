Field Guide

How the enquiry form, the CRM and the surveyor app fit together under Trevor's job process, agreed on 19 October 2026, with a walkthrough for each real use case.

How to use it: work through a use case doing exactly what the numbered steps say. Each step has an expected result. If what you see doesn't match, that's a gap to flag. "Known behaviours" at the end lists things that are correct but surprising, so you don't chase them. Trevor's step-by-step sign-off list is the separate THAC CRM Test Script page. This guide is the fuller reference behind it.

01Process at a glance

A job goes onto the map the moment it's real: when a customer accepts a quote, or when Trevor adds it. There's no approval step, and a surveyor's claim is final. After the visit the surveyor taps Survey Done, then Data Sent once the data is in SharePoint. Trevor finishes the job in one of three ways.

02Customer: the website form

enquiry-form/index.html and its linked pages. No login. Each action is one visit, started from the form or from a link in an email.

Submit a new survey enquiryenquiry-form/index.html

The main path: a customer describes a job and gets an instant, worked-out price.

  1. Open the form, choose New Survey, and pick a survey type: Stage 1, Stage 2, Tree Condition / Risk, Insurer / Mortgage, Subsidence, NHBC or Site Visit.
    Expect: a short description of that survey appears under the chosen card.
  2. Enter the tree count (a band, or the m² calculator) and the site postcode.
    Expect: the postcode is checked against the area you cover before you can continue. Out of area shows a message and stops you.
  3. Choose a deadline: 15 working days (no charge), 10 (+£75), 5 (+£150) or 3 (+£200).
    Expect: the price for each option updates as you change the tree count or deadline.
  4. Enter your contact details and submit.
    Expect: a TH #### reference is shown, and a quote email (subject starting 🌳) arrives within a minute or two. In the CRM a job appears as Awaiting go-ahead. It is not on the map yet. Trevor gets a "new enquiry" email.
Submit an amendment requestenquiry-form/index.html → Amend Existing Report

For changes to a report already delivered. Never priced automatically.

  1. Choose Amend Existing Report, enter the original job reference (e.g. TH 5291) and describe the change.
    Expect: the form says amendments are charged at £100 + VAT per hour (minimum £25 + VAT) and no automated quote is generated.
  2. Pick a deadline and submit.
    Expect: a confirmation, but no price and no quote email. The CRM shows an amendment job as Awaiting go-ahead. It never goes on the map.
Note: Trevor quotes amendments outside the system. When the work is done he uses Report & Invoice Sent on the job page, which is offered on amendments at any stage.
Enquire on behalf of a clientenquiry-form/index.html → on-behalf-of option

For introducers, agents and brokers enquiring for their own client.

  1. Tick the option to add your client's email address so they get a copy of the quote. It's unticked on every visit.
    Expect: two extra fields appear: Your Client's Name and Your Client's Email Address.
  2. Complete the rest of the form and submit.
    Expect: the quote email goes to the client and a copy goes to the introducer. The introducer is linked to the job in the CRM.
Accept a quoteaccept-quote.html → confirm-quote.html

This is where a website job becomes real and goes on the map.

  1. From the quote email, click Accept Quote.
    Expect: the quote details load (reference, survey type, postcode, deadline, price). In the CRM the job moves straight to Unallocated and appears as a red pin on the Live Map and in surveyors' apps within their area. The paying and report-to customers are linked to the job.
  2. Fill in billing contact, end client, report addressee and site access, then confirm.
    Expect: a success message. The job page shows Details Received. Trevor gets a "quote accepted" email saying the job is on the map and the database needs creating. The customer gets a second email linking to confirm-quote.html.
  3. (Optional) Follow that second link and add anything missed.
    Expect: only fields you fill in are saved. Nothing you gave before is blanked, and the job doesn't move again.
Known overlap: the accept page's second step and confirm-quote.html collect mostly the same details. Both are safe, but a customer who completes the first has nothing new to add on the second.
Decline a quoteaccept-quote.html
  1. From the quote email, click Decline and give a reason if you like.
    Expect: the enquiry is marked declined and the job is archived. It never goes on the map, and appears under the Complete tab labelled Archived (not Job Complete). Trevor gets an email with the reason.

03Office: the CRM

admin/ on Railway. Every action below needs a signed-in admin or user account. The ? button at the top right of every page opens the job journey guide, and hovering a stage badge shows what got the job there and what happens next.

Add a job yourselfJobs or Clients → New job · customer page → Add job
  1. Click New job. Pick an existing customer, or add one on the New customer tab. Customers are matched by email, so nobody is added twice.
    Expect: the reference box suggests the next free TH number. A reference already in use is refused.
  2. Choose survey type, site postcode, deadline and agreed fee. Leave Start as on On the map (Unallocated) and create the job.
    Expect: the job page opens as Unallocated, with urgency and SLA date worked out (working days, weekends skipped). It is a red pin on the map straight away. Trevor gets a "New job on map, database to create" email.
Database and report to-dosjob page → Progress · Jobs list · Dashboard → To Do

Trevor's two jobs on every live job. The database matters most: nothing can happen until it goes to the surveyor.

  1. On a new job, look at Database in the Progress card.
    Expect: "Database to create", in amber, with a hint saying to do it first. The dashboard counts it under Database to create, and under Database needed, surveyor waiting once a surveyor has claimed it.
  2. Once it's made, set Database to Created: digital plan or Created: JPEG, awaiting digital plan. The same dropdown is in the Jobs list and the dashboard table.
    Expect: the date is stamped. JPEG stays amber and is counted under Awaiting digital plan until switched to Digital plan. A JPEG database still counts as created for a waiting surveyor.
  3. Click Report to draft when you've drafted the report.
    Expect: it turns to "Report drafted" with the date. Clicking again asks before unticking.
Allocate a job to a named surveyorjob page → Allocate to Surveyor

Offers a job to one surveyor with a time limit, instead of waiting for someone to claim it.

  1. Click Allocate to Surveyor, choose the surveyor and a response window.
    Expect: an amber line "Allocated · Nh left" with a Change button. The surveyor sees the offer in the app (see the Respond to an allocation use case).
  2. The surveyor accepts in the app.
    Expect: the job is Allocated with their name, and Trevor gets the "Job allocated" email. If they reject it, the offer clears and the job stays on the map for anyone to claim.
Take a job off the map, or unassign a surveyorjob page → Actions
  1. On an Unallocated job, click Remove from Map.
    Expect: the job becomes Awaiting go-ahead and leaves the map. Put on Map puts it back as Unallocated.
  2. On an Allocated job, click Unassign Surveyor and give a reason.
    Expect: back to Unallocated with a red pin. Trevor gets a "Job back on map, surveyor handed it back" email with the reason. The same happens when a surveyor hands a job back from the app.
Edit job detailsjob page
  1. Edit trees, survey date, site access, parking, documents, the agreed fee or the introducer.
    Expect: each saves on its own and shows at once. Trees also updates the linked enquiry. A confirmed survey date shows green "Confirmed", otherwise orange "Proposed".
  2. Change Urgency in the Status card and click Save status.
    Expect: the SLA restarts from today at that tier's working days (Urgent 3, Elevated 5, Standard 10, Low 15).
Note: the Stage dropdown in the Status card sets the stage directly. It's for fixing mistakes. The normal moves happen through the buttons and the app, which also send the right emails.
Finish a job and invoice itjob page → Progress → Finish the job

Offered once the survey is done. Raise each invoice in QuickBooks first. The CRM records it for the dashboard totals and overdue flags.

  1. Click Report & Invoice Sent (Job Complete). Check the amount (the agreed fee is filled in) and save.
    Expect: the invoice is recorded and the job becomes Job Complete: off the main list, under the Complete tab. Sending the report and invoicing are one step. No separate "report sent" stage and no "invoice now" email.
  2. Or click Tree Data Sent, Part Invoiced. Half the fee is filled in. Save.
    Expect: invoice 1 is recorded and the job shows Awaiting Client · Part Invoiced, off the map but still listed. Its SLA is paused. Later, Report & Invoice Sent fills in the balance as invoice 2 and completes the job.
  3. Or click Tree Data Sent, Not Invoiced.
    Expect: Awaiting Client · Not Invoiced. Later you can part invoice it or complete it.
  4. When money arrives, click Mark Invoice Paid (or Invoice 1 / 2) in Actions.
    Expect: a green Paid badge on that invoice. An invoice unpaid after 30 days shows an Overdue flag.
Jobs waiting for plans, and Stage 2job page → Create Stage 2 Job · Plans Received
  1. On a two-stage Stage 1 job, click Create Stage 2 Job. Or add a job with Start as Waiting for plans.
    Expect: the job sits as Waiting for Plans: off the map, with no SLA date.
  2. When the plans arrive, click Plans Received, Activate Job.
    Expect: the SLA starts from today and the job goes straight onto the map as Unallocated.
Find jobs in the listJobs page
  1. Use the stage tabs: Go-ahead, Plans, Unallocated, Allocated, Survey Done, Data Received, Awaiting Client, Complete.
    Expect: each tab shows a count. All shows everything except completed jobs.
  2. Open Filters: urgency, To do (Database to create, Awaiting digital plan, Report to draft), surveyor and introducer.
    Expect: each active filter shows as a chip you can clear with ×. Dashboard counts link straight to the matching filtered list.
  3. Click a column header to sort, and Shift-click more headers to sort by several, e.g. surveyor, then survey date.
    Expect: small 1, 2, 3 badges on the sorted headers, like Excel's "sort by, then by".
Approve, pause or archive a surveyorsurveyors.html, surveyor-detail.html

Surveyors are the one thing Trevor approves. Their claims are not.

  1. Filter the surveyor list by Pending Approval to find new registrations.
    Expect: Trevor is also emailed when someone registers.
  2. Open one and click Approve & Activate, entering their hourly rate.
    Expect: they can sign in to the app at once and get an approval email. They see jobs within their radius of their home postcode.
  3. Use Pause Account for a temporary hold, or Archive to remove access.
    Expect: a paused surveyor sees no available jobs. Archiving also unlinks their login and stops their coverage counting.
Manage CRM usersusers.html
  1. Click Invite User and choose Admin or User.
    Expect: they get an email invite to set their own password.
  2. Use Disable / Enable or Remove Access on an existing account.
    Expect: you can't disable, demote or delete your own account.

04Surveyor: the mobile app

The app in surveyor-new/ (Heaps Arboriculture Surveyors on the App Store and Play Store). The Survey Done and Data Sent buttons arrive in version 1.0.8.

Register a surveyor accountRegister screen
  1. Tap Create an account and fill in contact, coverage area, insurance and compliance details.
    Expect: you're told the account is awaiting approval.
  2. Sign in before Trevor approves you.
    Expect: a "pending approval" screen. No jobs are visible yet.
Find and claim a jobJobs → Available · Map tab
  1. Open Available, or the Map tab.
    Expect: only jobs within your radius of your home postcode. On the map they're red pins, and your own allocated jobs are green. The pop-up for an available job shows its urgency.
  2. Open a job, tap Claim This Job and accept the NDA.
    Expect: it's yours straight away. No one approves the claim. It moves to My Jobs as "Next: visit site, then tap Survey Done", and leaves everyone else's map.
Respond to a job allocated to youJob detail → allocation banner
  1. Open a job Trevor allocated to you.
    Expect: a banner with the time left, and Claim / Reject buttons.
  2. Tap Claim (accepting the NDA), or Reject.
    Expect: claiming makes it yours exactly as a normal claim does. Rejecting clears the offer and leaves the job on the map for others.
Survey Done, Data Sent, and handing backJob detail · version 1.0.8
  1. Back from site, tap Survey Done.
    Expect: the pin leaves every map. The CRM shows "Survey done, awaiting data from surveyor". My Jobs says "Next: put the data in SharePoint, tap Data Sent".
  2. Put the tidied database, photos and site notes in SharePoint, then tap Data Sent.
    Expect: the CRM shows "Survey data received, report to complete" and Trevor gets an email. The app says there's nothing more to do, and the job leaves My Jobs.
  3. Before the survey, tap Hand Back Job to give a job up.
    Expect: it returns to the map as Unallocated, and Trevor is emailed. Hand Back isn't offered once Survey Done is tapped.
Older app versions: 1.0.7 and earlier have one button, Mark Field Data Uploaded. The database treats it as Survey Done and Data Sent together, so the job goes straight to Data received.
Profile, coverage, availability, insuranceProfile tab
  1. Update your home postcode and travel radius under Coverage Area.
    Expect: the matched outcodes are listed below, worked out from postcode and radius.
  2. Mark days on the Availability calendar by tapping or dragging.
    Expect: grey means no answer yet, not unavailable.
  3. Upload or replace a PI, PL or DBS certificate under Insurance.
    Expect: only you and the office can see or replace your certificates.
  4. Use Delete Account at the bottom of Profile if you leave.
    Expect: your login and certificates are removed. Past jobs and pay records stay for the office.

06Known behaviours, not bugs

Things that are correct as built but might look surprising while testing.

Every email is copied to nick@aut-ai.com, and customer replies go to Nick
Deliberate while testing. Set TESTING_CC to null and change REPLY_TO_ADDRESS to Trevor's address in email-templates.ts when going fully live.
The customer is never emailed their report by the system
Trevor sends the report and invoice himself, then records it with Report & Invoice Sent.
Older databases show "Created (plan not recorded)"
Databases ticked before the digital / JPEG choice existed. Pick Digital plan or JPEG to record it. The 76 imported allocated jobs started as "Database to create", so the dashboard's "surveyor waiting" count is high until Trevor sets the ones already done.
The app's Upload File button doesn't upload yet
It shows "Document picker will be enabled in the next release". Surveyors send their data through SharePoint, so the process doesn't depend on it.
The Live Map shows only red and green pins
Only Unallocated and Allocated jobs are shown. Every other job is off the map by design, and is found through the Jobs list.
"Report Sent" can still appear on a few old jobs
A stage from the previous process. New jobs never reach it. Use Report & Invoice Sent to finish those jobs.
A nested, unused GitHub Actions workflow file still exists
admin/.github/workflows/deploy-functions.yml never runs (GitHub only reads the top-level .github/). Harmless, but it can be deleted.
