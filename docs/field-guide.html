Field Guide

A working reference for how the enquiry form, admin CRM, and surveyor app fit together today — and a walkthrough for every real use case, written so you can test each one directly against what's actually in the code.

How to use this tomorrow: work through each use case in order, doing exactly what the numbered steps say. Each step has an expected result — if what you see doesn't match, that's precisely the kind of gap worth flagging back. The "Known behaviours" section at the end lists things that are correct-but-surprising, so you don't waste time on those.

01Process at a glance

Two separate paths from the same public form: a new survey gets an instant computed quote; an amendment to existing work never does — Trevor prices those by hand.

02Customer — public enquiry form

enquiry-form/index.html and its linked pages. No login; every action is a single anonymous visit driven by a link.

Submit a new survey enquiryenquiry-form/index.html

The core path: a customer describes a job and gets an instant, computed price.

  1. Open the form, choose New Survey, and pick a survey type (Stage 1, Stage 2, Tree Condition/Risk, Insurer/Mortgage, Subsidence, NHBC, or Site Visit).
    Expect: a short description of that survey type appears under the selected card.
  2. Enter tree count (a band, or use the m² calculator) and a site postcode.
    Expect: postcode is checked against coverage before you can continue — a message appears if it's out of area.
  3. Choose a deadline: 15+ days (free), 10 days (+£75), 5 days (+£150), or 3 days (+£200).
    Expect: the price for each option updates live as you change tree count/deadline. "No rush" no longer appears — it was retired in favour of the 15-day tier.
  4. Enter contact details and submit.
    Expect: a reference number (TH ####) is shown, and a quote email arrives within a minute or two, with the tree icon 🌳 leading the subject line.
Submit an amendment requestenquiry-form/index.html → "Amend an existing report"

For changes to a report THAC already delivered. Never auto-priced.

  1. Choose Amend Existing Report, enter the original job reference (e.g. TH 5291) and describe the change needed.
    Expect: the form explicitly states amendments are charged at £100+VAT/hour (min £25+VAT) and that no automated quote is generated.
  2. Pick a deadline (same tiers as new surveys, used only for SLA tracking — none carry a surcharge for amendments) and submit.
    Expect: a confirmation, but no price and no quote-accept email — Trevor reviews it directly in the CRM.
Note: there's currently no dedicated admin screen for "quote this amendment" — Trevor's response happens outside the system (email/phone) after seeing it land in the CRM as a new enquiry.
Submit on behalf of a clientenquiry-form/index.html → "I'm enquiring on behalf of someone else"

For introducers/agents/brokers submitting for their own client.

  1. Tick the on-behalf-of checkbox (unchecked by default on every page load — this doesn't persist between visits).
    Expect: three extra fields appear — introducer name, email, company.
  2. Complete the rest of the form as normal and submit.
    Expect: the quote email goes to the client's email and CCs the introducer's email.
Accept a quoteaccept-quote.html → confirm-quote.html

Two pages, back to back — see the callout below on why.

  1. From the quote email, click Accept Quote.
    Expect: the quote details load (reference, survey type, postcode, deadline, price).
  2. Fill in billing contact, end-client details, report addressee, and site access, then confirm.
    Expect: a success message, and — within a minute — a second, separate email inviting you to confirm-quote.html to add any remaining details.
  3. (Optional) Follow that second link and fill in the same class of details again if anything was missed the first time.
    Expect: this never overwrites what you already gave with blanks — only fields you actually fill in this time are saved.
Known overlap: accept-quote.html's own step 2 and confirm-quote.html collect substantially the same fields (billing/end-client/report/access details) — built independently, never consolidated. Both work correctly and are non-destructive of each other, but a customer who completes step 2 fully has nothing new to add on the second page. Worth a product decision on whether to keep sending the follow-up email at all.
Decline a quoteaccept-quote.html
  1. From the quote email, click Decline and optionally give a reason.
    Expect: the enquiry is marked declined and its linked job is automatically archived — it drops out of the admin pipeline entirely, no further action needed.

03Admin — CRM

admin/*.html. Every action below needs a logged-in admin or user account.

Approve an enquiry to the mapjob-detail.html

The one manual gate between "customer accepted" and "surveyors can see it." Accepting a quote does not do this automatically.

  1. Open the job (created the moment the enquiry was submitted — it exists in pending_approval from the very first step, well before any quote is accepted).
    Expect: tree count, postcode, and — once accepted — the customer's billing/site details are already visible.
  2. Click Approve to Map.
    Expect: dispatch state flips to red (live/unclaimed), a surveyor pay estimate is calculated if not already set, and a client row is created/linked automatically if this is the first time this contact has been seen.
Allocate a job directly to a surveyorjob-detail.html → "Allocate to Surveyor"

Bypasses the open marketplace — offers the job to one named surveyor with a response deadline.

  1. Click Allocate to Surveyor, choose a surveyor and a response window (default or custom hours).
    Expect: a countdown ("Allocated · Xh remaining") appears on the job.
Note: this is a real, working admin feature (RLS-correct as of this session) but currently has no matching screen in the surveyor app to accept or reject the offer — it's a database-and-CRM-only feature until that UI exists.
Edit tree count, financials, and site detailsjob-detail.html
  1. Use the Set/Edit button on Tree Count Band, Financials, Site Access, or Documents.
    Expect: each saves independently and immediately reflects in the page — tree count also updates the linked enquiry, not just the job.
Track report progressjob-detail.html → "Report Progress" card

Only appears once the surveyor has marked field data as uploaded.

  1. Tick Axiscape DB Prepared, then Report Drafted as each is done.
    Expect: a timestamp appears next to each.
  2. Tick Report Finalised.
    Expect: dispatch state advances to green, and — automatically, no extra click — the client receives their "report ready" email.
Raise and mark invoicesjob-detail.html
  1. Click Raise Invoice (or Invoice 1/2 for split billing), confirm the amount and date.
    Expect: for single invoicing, the job archives immediately; for split billing, only invoice 2 archives it.
  2. Click Mark as Paid once payment lands.
    Expect: paid date is recorded per invoice, independent of the archive step.
Approve, pause, or archive a surveyorsurveyor-detail.html, surveyors.html
  1. Filter the surveyor list by Pending Approval to find new self-registrations.
    Expect: you should also get an email the moment someone registers — this was broken until today and is now fixed.
  2. Open one and click Approve & Activate, entering their hourly rate.
    Expect: they can now log into the app immediately, and receive an approval email.
  3. Use Pause Account for a temporary hold, or Archive to remove access permanently and clear their postcode coverage.
    Expect: archiving also unlinks their login — they're locked out immediately, and their coverage stops counting toward any postcode being "served."
Manage CRM login usersusers.html
  1. Click Invite User, choose Admin or User role.
    Expect: they get an email invite to set their own password — you never see or set it.
  2. Use Disable/Enable or Remove Access on an existing account as needed.
    Expect: you can't disable, demote, or delete your own account — the option is blocked.

04Surveyor — mobile app

The actively developed app is surveyor-new/ (self-registration, insurance/compliance tracking, coverage-area management). surveyor-app/ is an earlier, simpler build — both talk to the same one database.

Register a new surveyor accountRegisterScreen
  1. Open the app, tap Create an account, and fill in contact, coverage area, insurance, and compliance details.
    Expect: submission succeeds and you're told your account is awaiting approval — this was fully broken as of yesterday and is fixed as of today.
  2. Try logging in before being approved.
    Expect: you land on a "pending approval" screen, not the job list — you cannot see or claim any job yet.
Browse and claim an available jobJobs → Available tab
  1. Once approved, open the Available tab.
    Expect: only jobs within your travel radius of your home postcode appear — this depends on the job having real coordinates, which is now set automatically on submission (previously it silently never was, for any job).
  2. Open a job and tap Claim This Job, accept the confidentiality agreement.
    Expect: your estimated pay is shown before you confirm, and the job moves to your My Jobs tab immediately after.
Respond to a job allocated to youJob Detail — allocated-job banner
  1. Open a job an admin has allocated directly to you.
    Expect: a red banner with a countdown, and Claim/Reject buttons.
  2. Tap Claim to accept it, or Reject to release it back for others.
    Expect: rejecting clears the allocation and returns the job to the open marketplace.
Field work and hand-backJob Detail
  1. Add field notes as you work, and tap Mark Field Data Uploaded once the visit is done.
    Expect: the office is notified automatically, and admin's Report Progress checklist becomes available on this job.
  2. If you need to release a job you've claimed, tap Hand Back.
    Expect: it returns to the open marketplace for another surveyor.
Not yet built: the file-upload button on a job ("Upload File") is a placeholder — tapping it just shows a "coming in the next release" message. There's no way to attach documents from the app yet.
Manage profile, coverage, availability, insuranceProfile tab
  1. Update your home postcode and travel radius under Coverage Area.
    Expect: a list of matched outcodes appears below, auto-computed from that postcode and radius.
  2. Mark yourself available/unavailable on the Availability calendar by tapping or dragging across days.
    Expect: days default to unavailable until you actively mark them — a grey day means "no answer yet," not "unavailable."
  3. Upload or replace a PI/PL/DBS certificate under Insurance.
    Expect: only you (and admin) can see or replace your own certificates — this was a real gap (any surveyor could read or overwrite anyone else's) closed today.
