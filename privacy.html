Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Heaps Arboriculture ("we", "us") provides tree survey, planning and consultancy services, through heapsarboriculture.co.uk, our public enquiry form, our admin system, and the Heaps Arboriculture Surveyors mobile app used by our surveyors. This policy covers all of them.

What we collect

WhoWhatWhy
Enquirers / customersName, email, phone, site address, enquiry and job detailsTo quote, arrange and carry out your survey, and keep a record of the work
Surveyors (employees/contractors)Name, email, phone, home postcode, insurance (PI/PL) and DBS details and certificates, qualifications, availabilityTo allocate jobs, confirm you're insured and qualified, and pay you
Surveyors, in the appDevice location, while the app is openTo show your position on the job map and confirm parking details on site — never collected in the background

How we use it

To provide quotes and carry out surveys; to allocate and manage jobs; to meet our insurance, safeguarding and professional obligations; and to send you emails about an enquiry, job or account you hold with us. We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Third parties we use to run the service: Supabase (database and file storage), Resend (sending emails) and Google (maps and geocoding). We don't share your data with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and job records are kept as part of our business records. If a surveyor deletes their account (see below), we remove their personal details and documents straight away; job and payment history stays on file with no name or contact details attached, since we're required to keep business records regardless.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Surveyors can delete their own account and data at any time from the app (Profile → Delete Account) or via our account deletion page. For anything else, or if you have a question about this policy, email trevor@heapsarboriculture.co.uk.
